1. Introduction

1.1 Project Overview

The GIFT City Connect Portal is a centralized, web‑based platform designed to be the definitive digital ecosystem for Gujarat International Finance Tec‑City (GIFT City). It aims to connect visitors, businesses, property owners, and administrators by providing a unified source of information and services. This includes listings for companies, offices, residential and commercial properties, educational institutions, and business support services.

1.2 Scope & Objectives

The primary objective is to build a robust, scalable, and user‑friendly platform that simplifies the discovery and management of resources within GIFT City.

  • Discovery – Enable users to easily search and filter for businesses, properties, and services.
  • Listing Management – Provide authenticated users (Business/Property Owners) the ability to manage their own listings.
  • Connectivity – Facilitate communication between service providers and potential clients/customers via contact forms and enquiry management.
  • Administration – Provide a powerful dashboard for the platform administrator to manage content, users, and monitor activity.

1.3 Target Audience

  • Visitors – Individuals exploring GIFT City for work, living, or investment opportunities.
  • Business Owners – Companies looking to establish a presence, promote their services, or find office space.
  • Property Owners – Individuals or firms with residential or commercial property to list for sale or rent.
  • Administrators – Platform managers responsible for maintaining the integrity and quality of data.

3. System Features & Modules

Companies & Offices

Comprehensive directory of companies and office spaces. Search by name/industry, view detailed profiles, and map locations.

Residential Properties

Apartments, flats, and houses. Filter by location, price, BHK, and amenities. High-res images and direct owner contact.

Commercial Spaces

Retail shops, warehouses, land plots. Zoning info, utilities, and rental terms. Map-based visualization.

Schools & Education

Educational institutions with curriculum details (CBSE, ICSE, IB). Filter by board, grade level, and location.

Business Support

Marketplace for legal, accounting, IT, HR, and catering services. Profiles with direct enquiry/booking.

4. Functional Requirements

4.1 Core Features

  • Global Search – Powerful search bar indexing all modules.
  • Advanced Filters – Refine results by category, price, location, amenities, ratings.
  • Interactive Maps – Listings shown on map; click pins for summary cards.
  • User Registration/Login – Secure JWT‑based authentication.
  • Responsive Design – Optimised for desktop, tablet, and mobile.

4.2 Admin Dashboard

  • Content Management – CRUD operations on all collections.
  • User Management – Activate, deactivate, or delete user accounts.
  • Analytics Dashboard – Visual reports on views, user growth, enquiry traffic.
  • Report Generation – Export reports on listings and user activity.

4.3 User Interaction

  • Contact Enquiry – Form on listing pages to message owners.
  • Listing Management – Authenticated users can add, edit, remove own listings.
  • Review System – Users can leave ratings and reviews (admin‑moderated).
Highlight: All core features are designed to ensure a seamless user journey from discovery to conversion.

5. Non‑Functional Requirements

5.1 Performance

RequirementTarget
Page Load Time≤ 3 seconds (landing & listing pages)
Concurrent UsersSupport ≥ 1,000 concurrent users
ScalabilityHorizontal scaling via load balancers

5.2 Security

  • Authentication – All protected endpoints require valid JWT.
  • Input Validation – Server‑side validation to prevent injection attacks.
  • Password Hashing – Using bcrypt (salt rounds ≥ 10).
  • Media Security – Temporary access tokens for private files.

5.3 Usability

  • Intuitive UI – Clean, modern design with minimal learning curve.
  • Accessibility – Reasonable compliance with WCAG 2.1 AA.
  • User Feedback – Clear loading states, success/error messages, and tooltips.
Note: Security and performance are non-negotiable priorities for production deployment.
